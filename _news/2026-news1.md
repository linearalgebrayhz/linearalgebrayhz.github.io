Our paper "TKCAM: Text and Keyframe to Camera Trajectory Generation" has been accepted to NeurIPS 2026 as a poster. The [paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11105), [code](https://github.com/linearalgebrayhz/TKCAM), and [project page](/projects/TKCAM/) are available.
